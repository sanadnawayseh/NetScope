        Measurement definitions

        RTT uses exact cycle means; bounded and unavailable values are excluded. Median needs one exact sample, p95 needs 20 and p99 needs 100. Trends need five valid samples in each window. Sampled availability is not continuous uptime. Recovery spans include observation gaps; unresolved ages are current, regardless of the selected period.
